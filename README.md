📌 Use Case:
 PM2.5 refers to particulate matter/air pollutant with a diameter of 2.5 micrometers or less.These fine particles can penetrate deep into the lungs and even the bloodstream,posing significant health risks.

Sources of PM2.5 include:
1) Vehicle exhaust(car,trucks).
2) Factories and power plants.
3) Burning of wood,coal or crop stubble
4) Wildfires
5) Indoor activities like smoking or cooking with solid fuels.

Health risks associated with PM2.5:
1) Respiratory problems(asthma,bronchitis).
2) Heart Disease.
3) Stroke.
4) Premature death in people with existing heart or lung conditions.
